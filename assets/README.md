## Key verified observations from the uploaded footage
The clips visibly depict sprinklers operating at residential lawns and newly prepared properties; watering of a planting area; stone and planting-bed landscaping; rooftop green-surface work; excavation and site preparation at residential properties; and vehicles/equipment. Some clips show commercial-looking settings, but the footage alone does not establish contracting arrangements or services that require certifications.

**Important:** These scenes occur in videos supplied by the user and associated with GreenMan's TikTok; this does not by itself confirm GreenMan completed every visible component. Ask the owner to approve project claims and publishing rights.

## Website placement recommendation
- Hero section: use an authentic original **landscape** photo if one can be obtained. None of these 512px-wide vertical frames are ideal for a large desktop hero.
- Homepage service cards: use active residential sprinkler, stone-and-plant border, and a residential yard-preparation photo.
- Irrigation page: sprinkler scenes from videos 1, 3 and 8.
- Landscaping page: green roof, stone beds and planting work from video 5.
- Gallery: arrange by irrigation, landscaping, site preparation and operations; do not present a 'before and after' pair unless it is demonstrably the same site.
- Do not use the footage of a flooded/wet parking area to advertise a specific service without owner verification.

## Technical caveats
All screenshots retain the original creator/player marks baked into the TikTok exports. For final commercial publishing, request the original high-resolution camera photos/videos, publication permission, and matching captions. Do not upscale these frames and pretend they are high resolution. Use explicit intrinsic size and object-fit cropping as appropriate. Logos/character art appearing in social media imagery may require review before using in website branding.
